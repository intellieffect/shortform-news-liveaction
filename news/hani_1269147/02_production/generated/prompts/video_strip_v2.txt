The small red dwarf star at the top flares up and its glow swells into a broad, wide, diffuse wash of reddish light and hot glowing particles that spreads downward over the whole width of the frame like a wide wind, not a narrow beam. When this wide wind reaches the rocky planet at the bottom, the planet's thin bluish atmospheric halo is gently pushed off the star-facing side and streams away downward behind the planet as a long faint pale tail, while the rocky body stays intact and unchanged.
