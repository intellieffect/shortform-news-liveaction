Start from this image and keep both bodies where they are. The small red dwarf star at the top brightens and sends a steady stream of reddish glowing radiation downward toward the rocky planet at the bottom. When it reaches the planet, the planet's thin bluish atmospheric halo is pushed away from the star and pulled out into a long faint tail behind the planet, streaming down and away from the star, while the rocky body itself stays intact and unchanged. No explosion, no fire on the planet, no text. Slow, continuous, cinematic, photoreal.
